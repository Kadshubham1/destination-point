About Us

Who We Are

Pointing You in the Right Direction

Destination Point is an organisation committed to helping both businesses and individuals reach their goals through two focused areas of expertise: digital marketing and counselling & guidance.

We believe every person and every organisation has a destination in mind. Our role is to provide the clarity, tools, and support needed to get there — whether that means growing your brand's digital presence or finding the right personal or career direction.

Our approach is rooted in understanding your unique needs. We take the time to listen, assess, and offer direction that is practical, honest, and meaningful.
